# Role-Specific Interview Signals

## Software Engineer
- Strong signals: root-cause debugging, system trade-offs, testing/rollback guardrails, observability metrics (p95 latency, error rate, uptime), and calm cross-team incident coordination.

## Product Designer
- Strong signals: user research insights shifting direction, scope trade-offs protecting core UX, accessibility (keyboard/screen reader), design system consistency, and post-launch activation/usability metrics.

## Product Manager
- Strong signals: ruthless prioritization criteria, saying no with data, launch risk mitigation, engineering/design alignment, and pairing leading and lagging metrics.

## Data Analyst
- Strong signals: metric hygiene and reconciliation, handling messy or incomplete data with explicit caveats, experiment guardrails, automation time saved, and decision-first storytelling.

## Frontend Developer
- Strong signals: rendering performance profiling (LCP/INP, re-renders, bundle size), accessible custom widgets (ARIA, focus management), responsive edge-case partnership with design, and regression testing.

## Customer Success
- Strong signals: de-escalating tense renewals, structured triage across accounts, partnering with Product/Support on root causes, onboarding time-to-value, and retention/NPS outcomes.

# STAR Framework & Rubric Anchors

## STAR Method
- **Situation**: Concise context, constraints, and stakes (1–2 sentences).
- **Task**: Your specific responsibility or target outcome.
- **Action**: Concrete steps *you* personally led ("I designed...", "I aligned..."), explaining trade-offs.
- **Result**: Measurable outcome (metrics, latency, error reduction, time saved, adoption, or lesson learned).

## Scoring Anchors (0.0 - 10.0)
- **9.0 - 10.0 (Outstanding)**: Crisp STAR flow, explicit personal ownership, concrete trade-offs, and quantified impact.
- **7.5 - 8.9 (Strong)**: Clear structure and ownership; minor opportunity to sharpen metrics or collaboration details.
- **6.0 - 7.4 (Solid Foundation)**: Relevant story, but vague on personal actions, constraints, or measurable results.
- **Below 6.0 (Needs Work)**: Off-track, overly brief, purely "we" without personal ownership, or missing an outcome.

## Coaching Upgrades
- Replace "helped improve performance" with numbers (e.g., "cut p95 latency by 42%").
- Replace "we fixed it" with your exact role and how you coordinated with teammates.
